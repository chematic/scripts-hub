How do I post a card?+
Message @vhb7 on Discord and send your script, the game name, a short description, the features you want listed, and your redirect link. If your access is approved, you can create the card from the dashboard.
How do I get dashboard access?+
Message @vhb7 and ask for access. You may be asked for the HWID and public IP from your machine so the device can be authorized.
How do I get my HWID?+
Open the dashboard and use Copy HWID command. Paste the command into Command Prompt or PowerShell. It prints the same 64 character HWID format used by the hub and copies it to your clipboard.
Why is the HWID needed?+
The hub uses it as part of the device access check. It helps keep creator and admin actions tied to an authorized machine.
Why is my IP needed?+
The public IP is an additional device check. If your connection changes, your stored device access may need to be updated.
How many cards can a creator make?+
Creator access currently allows 2 cards per day. Admin access is not limited by that creator quota.
Can I edit my card?+
Yes. Open the dashboard, choose your card, edit its details and save. A creator can edit cards that belong to that creator.
Can I delete a card?+
Yes. A creator can delete their own card. Admins can manage cards across the hub.
What do Live, Updated, Beta and Offline mean?+
Live means the card is available, Updated highlights a recent change, Beta indicates a testing release, and Offline means the card is not currently available.
How do I change the card colors?+
In the card editor, choose a gradient and then choose both Accent Start and Accent End. The preview updates instantly.
How do seasonal themes work?+
Use the palette button in the header. Night is the default, with Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter variations. Your choice is saved on the current browser.
Why is my card not showing?+
Check the card status, refresh the hub, and confirm the redirect and image URLs are valid. If the card was just published, a page refresh can be needed.
How do I contact Azuno?+
Use the Discord link in the header or copy @vhb7 from the creator section.
Does Azuno run Roblox?+
Azuno is an independent script hub. It is not presented as an official Roblox service.
